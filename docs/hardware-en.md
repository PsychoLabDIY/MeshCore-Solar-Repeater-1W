# Hardware

The verified assembly is a Seeed XIAO nRF52840 with Wio-SX1262 and an 868 MHz antenna. The solar input is a 1 W DIY lamp panel; one 18650 cell was used, while capacity, protection and state of health remain unverified.

A future 1S2P pack must use matched cells, equalized voltage, a proper 1S protection circuit and a compatible CC/CV charger. Never parallel cells at different voltages. The wiring diagram is conceptual only.
