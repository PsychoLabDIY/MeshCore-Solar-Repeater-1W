# Firmware

The recorded device runs MeshCore `v1.18.0 (Build: 15 Oct 2026)` on `Seeed Xiao-nrf52`. USB QA confirmed 869.618 MHz, SF8, BW 62.5 kHz, CR8, RXPS Balanced level 6, preamble 16, and `err=0` while armed.

RXPS timing is not a current measurement and does not prove zero packet loss. The platform `powersaving` flag is kept separate from RXPS. Firmware binaries, keys and device preferences are intentionally not redistributed. Use upstream MeshCore for source and release provenance.
