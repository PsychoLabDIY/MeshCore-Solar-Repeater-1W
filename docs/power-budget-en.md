# Power budget

These are models, not current measurements. One 3.7 V, 2000 mAh cell is roughly 7.4 Wh nominal; using 80% gives about 5.92 Wh before conversion losses and protection cut-off. A 1 W panel at 0.5/1/2 peak-sun-hours and 50–70% efficiency yields roughly 0.25–1.40 Wh/day.

At a hypothetical 20 mA average battery current, 3.9 V × 0.020 A × 24 h is about 1.87 Wh/day. The real device current is unmeasured, so overnight autonomy is not guaranteed.
